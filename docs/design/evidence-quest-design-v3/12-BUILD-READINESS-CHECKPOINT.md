## 3. Shared trace rules and the exact information

The following are **written contract traces**, checked against Items 05–10 and the acceptance inputs. They are not browser or player tests. A denotes a legal avatar approach in Item 05 coordinates. CT IDs always resolve to Item 07 or the five-entry technical addendum; text below is not a new catalog.

Every physical row uses UI.WORLD.MOVING → UI.WORLD.OPERATING and T.WORLD.ARRIVE/T.WORLD.COMMIT unless specified otherwise. Movement is legal pathfinding; choosing a remote room follows its real doors. T.WORLD.RETARGET/CANCEL cancels uncommitted intent; T.WORLD.ABORT obeys the object's actual before/after endpoint. Opening a supporting view cancels pending travel and releases held movement keys. Close/Back/Escape restores the previous supporting owner or surviving world object/ACC.OBJECTS, never automatically resumes motion or a cue.

Exposure columns below are **increments**, not replacements for previous exposure. No assistance exists unless a requested hint/direct response is actually displayed. Inspecting a note exposes its visible spans, not proof of understanding. Private typing changes a draft, Record changes a record, addressed delivery changes received evidence, and explicit Help sends a request; these are separate actions.
