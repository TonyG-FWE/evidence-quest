## 5. Make usable objects recognizable and proportionate

Agreed common rule: a usable world object receives a clear highlight and named action when targeted, with scenery visually distinct from things the child can operate. A lantern offers the existing **Read story**; a loose bridge end uses the shown **Fasten this end**. Exact cue styling, targeting, unavailable-action behavior, and non-pointer access remain R15-R17 work. D033-D035's goal and named destination guidance remains available.

The game must apply the right interaction model: Pip physically approaches people and carried-item handoffs; the child directs the paper boats/attachments in construction mode. A distant object label cannot complete a conversation or delivery. Text word-help interaction remains separately available under its existing agreements; this world-object cue rule does not limit clickable word coverage. Keyboard/focus equivalents, touch, contrast, prompt persistence, and unavailable-action explanations remain R15-R16.

Object sizes should explain their use: the single garden boat cannot carry Pip; joined tops form a visibly plausible walking route; passenger boats are recognizably separate work vessels; pages and seed are carryable; the backpack fits Pip; benches, workbench, and plants fit the cast and leave clear walkways. Moving an object must not change its size or cause unrelated props to rescale. Exact ratios, collision geometry, attachment clearance, and camera inspection require a later measured visual reference and playable review. This draft provides no numeric geometry or asset approval.
