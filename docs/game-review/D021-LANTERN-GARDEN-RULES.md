## The decision

Agreed direction: the garden keeps its older stories. Its quiet period means fewer visits and fewer new contributions. Renewed sharing adds new stories and pictures, while older contributions remain available.

This resolves the earlier open fading/disappearance question by retaining stories and their illustrations. D010's unadopted flickering/fading examples do not become a memory-loss or story-fragment recovery mechanic. No exact length of the decline is introduced, and the decline still begins before the storm.
