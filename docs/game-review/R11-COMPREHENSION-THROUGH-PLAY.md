## What can be observed

The accepted exchanges distinguish the child's own explanation or wording from a supplied answer, a response after a hint and uncertain speech interpretation. Accept equivalent supported wording and plausible text-grounded inferences. Choosing a successful plan or illustration alone does not establish why it was chosen. A supported explanation is evidence of that response in that context; it does not by itself establish improvement or general mastery.

Keep original sources, possible interpretations and actual NPC reports separate. A hint shown to the player does not automatically tell an NPC the same fact. Preserve genuine alternatives, source revisiting and continuation; no required mistake, clue count, exact phrase or comprehension score is introduced.
