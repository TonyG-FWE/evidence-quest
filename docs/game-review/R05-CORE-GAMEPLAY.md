## What the player does

The child guides Pip through the garden adventure. They investigate why the garden has grown quiet, construct a usable crossing, help prepare stories, arrange how people participate, and see the resulting gathering and ending. They also act as director when arranging paper pieces or rehearsing a story, and may practise narration. Loop is ready for the brief studio framing and the resulting ending presentation.

The recurring pattern is: understand a current goal, inspect or read useful information, choose and act, observe the consequence, and revise or continue. This is not a compulsory source-opening sequence. Reading support, silent experimentation, prepared alternatives, and optional narration remain available under their agreed conditions.

## Freedom and meaningful alternatives

- The first route is guided, with supported exploration and visit-order variation. A suggestion does not require opening a source or choosing a particular answer before travel.
- A suitable crossing can succeed on the first attempt. Visible unsuccessful construction can be revised without losing the seed, page, or earlier conversation.
- The original direct and seed-first arrangements, including all five equally valid physical successes, remain protected. Their complete direct-action/ferry mapping is still a technical and interaction dependency; this summary does not claim that geometry is settled or implemented.
- Sol may use a prepared ending, a supported player-written ending emphasizing baking, thanks, or both, or his original draft for discussion. His chosen contribution is retained, and an unfinished story can still be a valid contribution.
- Mara may read at a later gathering, listen while Pip reads later, or remain at work while Pip shares her story at the usual time. The usual route includes the agreed later delivery of Grandma's story. No route is morally or educationally ranked above the others.
- Before Begin, the gathering can be replanned under D024's actual invitation/update rules. After it starts, time and invitations are fixed for that event while the agreed storytelling choices continue.
- Word help, extra reading/questions, and oral practice remain optional. A lookup, selected answer, completed story, or animation is not a reading score or learning-gain measurement.

## How the literacy purpose participates in play

Comprehension has a practical role in interpreting a construction instruction, understanding why a crossing does not resolve Mara's work conflict, connecting Sol's repair with its consequences, and arranging an applicable time and reader. These tasks create opportunities to use the text; their completion alone cannot establish understanding.

Vocabulary support explains a word in its actual passage and offers pronunciation. Secure describes the fastening action; obligation describes Mara's responsibility; postpone/postponed is used when proposing and communicating a later gathering. Full word coverage and later reuse remain R12 work.

Optional fluency practice has a story purpose: rehearse dialogue, an invitation, the chosen ending, or the final narration that the child may perform. Exact modeling, phrasing, audio/access, and feedback remain R10/R17 work. No assessed microphone performance is required by these agreements.

The specific agreed AI role is interpretation and focused revision help for Sol's player-written ending within defined story events and performed actions. Provider selection, context/schema, ambiguity, fallback, data, state authority, and evaluation remain R13/R20 work. The runtime's current authored response bank is not evidence that this role works.
