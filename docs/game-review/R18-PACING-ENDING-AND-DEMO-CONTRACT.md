# R18: Pacing, payoff, replay and the three-minute recording

Specification revision 1, September 14, 2026. SPECIFIED_DERIVED from D018-D026, D029-D043 and D080. Preserve the one complete chapter and its valid alternatives. The recording is a concise demonstration of that game; it is not a three-minute limit on the child's play.

## Rhythm and purpose

The chapter alternates useful reading with a decision/action and a visible consequence: understand the planting promise; discover Mara's constraint; build and use the crossing; plant together; work with Sol's actual story; make the gathering arrangement happen; share and record stories; complete the selected closing; present the earned ending. The same world carries each consequence forward.

Use the already agreed main route for the recording, without making it the only supported route: later gathering, Mara attends and reads, Sol develops an ending, word/reading help demonstrated, then the actual gathering and final narration. Preserve Grandma-first, Sol-first, Pip-reading, working-Mara and unfinished-Sol paths.

Initial pacing estimates for build review, not observed results or reading timers:

| Part | Approximate allowance | What earns the time |
|---|---|---|
| Studio/opening and first goal | 1 minute | Understand Pip's promise, enter the adventure, start moving |
| Mara and crossing | 4-6 minutes | Read/ask about her work, inspect the physical rule, choose a location, join/fasten and cross |
| Planting and first garden context | 2-3 minutes | Keep the actual promise, understand lanterns, share/report only what is available |
| Sol's story and ending | 4-6 minutes | Read the repair, obtain later facts, write/rehearse/revise or keep a meaningful draft |
| Gathering arrangement and event | 4-6 minutes | Real invitations, actual time/arrival consequences, contributions and Grandma's changed understanding |
| Closing and presentation | 2-4 minutes | Actual applicable delivery, lantern moment, four-part ending and optional reading feedback |

These estimates guide a compact chapter near the earlier 20-30-minute ambition without filler. Players may finish faster or spend longer with help. Measure real traversal and reading instead of forcing a duration. Shorten repeated transitions, redundant already-known dialogue and unnecessary backtracking; do not remove substantive sources or secretly complete required actions.

There is no before-dark countdown. Story time advances at Begin the gathering after actual preparation. Reading, changing settings, help, retries and pausing do not make the player miss an event. NPCs can remain available in the appropriate phase so a delayed reader is not punished.

## Actual completion and payoff

Completion needs the planted seed, Mara's story contribution through a valid arrangement, Sol's finished or open contribution, Grandma's actual shared account, Pip's recorded real moment and the chosen closing. The working-Mara path ends after delivery of Grandma's actual copy at the dock; it does not require an extra return to Grandma or claim that Mara read the delivered copy.

The garden visibly retains older stories and adds the actual new contributions. Sol's open draft is worthwhile and uses its original repair illustration. Mara need not attend for her story to matter. The final four-paragraph presentation follows the actual three-by-three Mara/Sol combination from D042 and never chooses a more impressive result than the one played.

Allow post-completion viewing/rereading and replaying the earned presentation. **Start a new adventure** is a separate explicit new local save, with a clear confirmation; it does not overwrite an existing adventure silently. Do not add a new-game-plus campaign, score chase or artificial daily-return system. The natural replay purpose is exploring an already-supported different arrangement or contribution.

## Recording plan: maximum three minutes

Record genuine gameplay and label cuts as transitions within the playthrough when needed. A chapter can be played fully before editing its recording. Developer setup/checkpoints may assist recording preparation but cannot be presented as player actions or successful live AI responses. Every shown result needs the demonstrated source/draft/actual state.

| Video segment | Target time | Visible proof |
|---|---|---|
| Purpose and entry | 0:00-0:20 | Age/reading goal described briefly; Loop-ready studio and Pip's clear objective |
| Read and act | 0:20-0:55 | Mara's meaningful constraint, optional word help, real boat placement/fastening and Pip crossing |
| Child contribution | 0:55-1:40 | Sol's source and actual typed ending; live interpreted feedback, visible rehearsal and one meaningful revision |
| Consequence | 1:40-2:15 | The actual later gathering with the correct people/stories; garden reflects the played contribution |
| Literacy feedback | 2:15-2:45 | Short explicit recording, model/replay or actual supported feedback, and a separate meaning question |
| Earned ending | 2:45-3:00 | Correct four-part outcome and concise statement of what was demonstrated |

These are editing targets, not a claim that each interaction runs in that time. If live service feedback is unavailable during recording, show its real fallback and identify the limitation. Do not substitute an authored response and call it live AI. The full playable repo/local application remains the underlying deliverable; the recording alone is not the game.
