## Agreed explanation of adaptive writing feedback

AI receives the original story, the later facts actually supplied by Sol, and the child's current ending. It adapts what it comments on, what help it offers, and the wording of its response to the meaning of that draft.

| Example child draft | Agreed illustrative feedback | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| My repair kept the flour dry, so Rina could bake the bread she had promised. | You've shown how keeping the flour dry helped Rina keep her promise. | Acknowledge the specific supported connection |
| I fixed the roof, so the rain stopped. | The repair stopped water getting into the bakery. It didn't stop the rain outside. Look again at what Rina needed to keep dry. | Address the specific causal misunderstanding; retains D031's correction |
| After I fixed the roof, everything was better. | What could Rina do after you fixed the roof? Adding that detail could help your reader understand why the repair mattered. | Offer a specific way to develop a vague ending |

These are authored illustrations of the desired feedback, not actual generated outputs or mandatory verbatim model responses. The child need not produce these drafts or make a mistake. D053's brief accurate thanks ending remains valid; optional development does not become an answer gate or require all endings to use the baking emphasis.

After a revision, feedback addresses the new draft. If the rain misunderstanding is corrected and the ending explains that the flour stayed dry, recognize that correction and stop repeating the obsolete rain explanation. Request/version concurrency handling remains a later technical dependency.

Interpret equivalent meanings without requiring particular keywords. The demonstrated paraphrases are "Keeping the flour dry allowed Rina to bake." and "Her flour wasn't ruined, so she could still make everyone's bread." Both express the same supported connection.

Choosing the existing baking, later thank-you visit, or both rehearsal scenes is a separate function from giving educational feedback. Current-draft adaptation does not establish ongoing personalization based on a child's learning history. No model retraining, difficulty schedule or learning assessment is inferred from this agreement.
