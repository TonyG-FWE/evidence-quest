## Purpose and fixed history

The child guides Pip through an interactive paper-story adventure about a lantern garden where people share stories. Pip has promised to plant a lantern seed with Grandma before dark. The garden has become quiet because Mara's changed work hours conflict with the gatherings and Sol doubts anyone would enjoy his ordinary, unfinished stories. Grandma interprets fewer visits as lost interest. These difficulties began before the storm, which later washed away the footbridge.

The child helps find ways for both friends' stories to be shared again. Mara can participate through her page while at work, read herself after work, or listen after work while Pip reads. Sol can share either supported finished ending or a draft, which can be developed during the gathering or remain open. These are equal successful contributions. The child changes how the characters respond to their circumstances; the underlying past events stay consistent.

The garden retains its older stories and illustrations. Available rooted lantern flowers receive new contributions. Pip's actually planted seed supplies his own flower, which later records an actual adventure moment. A picture choice changes the story's illustrated focus, not its events or author.

## Supported route and knowledge variations

- Pip may speak to Mara before crossing or visit Grandma first. The latter route plants first, offers the optional question about Grandma's interpretation, and later brings back Mara's real explanation and page. [D025](D025-GRANDMA-BEFORE-MARA.md).
- Pip may visit Sol before Grandma after crossing. Sol uses the direct opening, keeps the selected contribution, and Pip later reports it explicitly. Grandma need not send him back to ask a question already answered and reported. [D023](D023-VISIT-ORDER-AND-CHARACTER-KNOWLEDGE.md).
- The seed may be sent ahead before Pip crosses, or travel with him through the supported arrangement. A sent seed is with Grandma, while Pip and any held page remain on the starting bank until he crosses. The original five successful physical arrangements remain equal. [D018](D018-ROUTE-CROSSING-AND-TIME.md).
- The gathering may use usual time/Pip, later/Mara, or later/Pip. Mara reading at the usual time conflicts with her work and receives the agreed explanation and options. Prior page delivery to Grandma is not a universal requirement when Mara will tell her own story. [D017](D017-PLANNING-THE-GATHERING.md), D026.
- Plans can change before the event. Actual recipients receive the changed information; completed story work persists. The demonstrated time/invitation boundary begins with the gathering. [D024](D024-CHANGING-THE-GATHERING-PLAN.md).
- Grandma's knowledge does not update from a remote visit alone. Reports, page possession, delivery, story sharing, attendance, assistance, and completion remain distinct. Her later question to Sol supplies his previously private reason. D020, D023-D025.

## Chapter completion and optional activity

The completed chapter includes the actual planting, both Mara's and Sol's contributions, the gathering and Grandma's story, Pip's actual adventure moment, and the selected closing route. A deliberately omitted main contribution leaves the chapter in progress with a relevant next action. Partial endings that omit a main contribution are not selected for this demo.

Mara does not have to attend physically. Sol does not have to finish his manuscript. Extra questions, older stories, word help, rereading, aloud practice, and extra comparisons remain optional. Reading scores, source-opening counts, prescribed correct answers, and assessed speech do not gate progress. Completing a story action establishes neither independent reading nor literacy improvement.

Preparation comes before time advancement and guest arrival. Sharing happens during the gathering. The absent-Mara delivery occurs after Grandma shares her story. These distinctions prevent circular prerequisites or invented events. No current runtime change is claimed.
