# R14: Difficulty and help that preserve the game

Specification revision 1, September 14, 2026. SPECIFIED_DERIVED from D026, D030, D044-D079 under D080. The challenge is deciding and acting on the story/world; reading help reduces obstacles to participating. No new curriculum, difficulty menu or learner diagnosis is introduced.

## Common support rules

Help is available on request, including before an error. A child may reread, hear a model, ask for a small hint, open a fuller explanation or continue where the existing story rules permit. They need not use the levels in order. Opening help never writes an answer, selects an ending, performs an action or supplies independent-learning evidence.

Keep the full age-appropriate source and challenging vocabulary. Do not silently replace it with an easier version based on hesitation, speed, lookup count or an AI label. Adapt the immediate feedback to the current answer or explicit request as already agreed. Relevant story facts must be exposed through the real source; hint text cannot reveal an unencountered character motive.

Use one visible suggestion at a time. Stop repeating a resolved suggestion after revision. Mistakes do not consume items, remove progress, subtract points or force a replay. Physical feedback is immediate and factual; its helpful explanation is separate. No timer identifies a child as stuck or advances the gathering while they read.

## Evidence distinction

The local activity may record that a hint, full explanation, model or example was opened so feedback does not falsely call a later repetition independent. Do not create a longitudinal child profile. Store only the minimum assistance marker needed for the current result; game completion does not inspect it. R20 reports software support and any observed answer separately from literacy improvement.
