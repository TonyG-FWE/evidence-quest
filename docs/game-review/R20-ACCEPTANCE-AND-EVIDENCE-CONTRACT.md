| Stage | Required evidence | What passing means |
|---|---|---|

| Visual/play review | D028's first 3D encounter observed and accepted by Tony; later complete chapter inspected in motion | The appearance, proportions and interaction feel meet the intended game direction |
| Live service qualification | Actual authorized text/speech outputs on fixed varied cases, latency and failure evidence, provider/configuration identity | The enabled service path worked on these tested cases; no general child-accuracy claim |
| Usability/learning | Separately authorized first-time observation or learner study with methods/results recorded | Only the claims supported by that actual evaluation |

Do not require participant research to pretend it happened under the deadline. If no learner study is run, describe the product as providing reading practice, comprehension opportunities and vocabulary support; do not claim measured improvement, mastery, age certification or therapeutic effectiveness.

## Definition of ready to demonstrate

The whole selected route launches locally, clearly explains its purpose, performs its actions, retains its sources and actual outcomes, and ends correctly. Required alternate outcomes are checked. No critical progression, state, input or data-loss failures remain. Text/help/models fit the actual source. The final illustrated presentation is visibly coherent. The live AI/speech features being claimed have genuine evidence; their fallbacks are also demonstrated. Recording/repo instructions can be followed without hidden developer intervention or committed secrets.

If only the first encounter is implemented, call it the first encounter. If speech or AI is still unavailable, identify that limitation; a fallback is useful but not proof of the missing feature. Final delivery/submission remains a separate authorized action.
