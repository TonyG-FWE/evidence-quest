## Question and agreed approach

How does Grandma welcome Pip, introduce the garden's problem, and respond to Mara's later account when the child visits the garden first?

Agreed approach: keep the planting promise, let Grandma describe her own observation and interpretation, and make Mara's actual explanation available through the later dock conversation. The child can ask how Grandma reached her conclusion, offer to visit Mara, or explore another reachable place. The question is optional and does not unlock travel or establish comprehension.
