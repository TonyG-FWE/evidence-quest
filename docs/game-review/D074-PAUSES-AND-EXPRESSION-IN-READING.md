## The agreed response to a supported pause finding

Only if the actual recording supports that observation, highlight the full stop after come and show:

> Try a short pause after "come." The next sentence tells us what the boy promised.

This is one prepared practice suggestion after the reading attempt. It does not interrupt the child while they speak or add a pronunciation correction and an expression judgment at the same time.

Available actions in this example:

- **Hear my reading** plays the child's actual attempt.
- **Hear this part** plays a prepared reading of the exact three-sentence excerpt, including a natural sentence boundary after come.
- **Read it again** offers another reading of the same excerpt; any recording still requires an explicit listening start.
- **Back to the story** returns to the same source page and ongoing activity.

The child can listen to both readings, try the pause or continue playing. No fixed silence length, mandatory reread or restart of the adventure is introduced. A new observation after rereading must use the new audio, not the fact that Read it again was selected.
