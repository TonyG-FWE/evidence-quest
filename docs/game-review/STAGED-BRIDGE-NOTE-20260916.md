## Grandma’s maintenance note

These are my two reusable footbridge sections. Six posts and two ropes are beside them. The posts support the ropes along the two sides of the walkway. Keep the middle clear for walking. —Grandma

Look for a crossing where both sections together can reach the banks. Place the first section against the near bank. Seat its two near corner posts and its two center posts in their marked sockets.

Secure a rope along each side of the first section, from a near corner post to a center post. Wrap each rope around its posts, pull it taut and tie a knot. Both sides must be secure before you put your weight on this section.

Walk onto the secured first section. Bring the second section into place and connect the short ends. Seat the two far corner posts. Extend each rope from its center post to the matching far corner post, then wrap, tighten and tie it.

A section can reach the bank and still be loose. If you step onto an unsecured section, it may slip. Keep the secured part in place and recover the loose materials. You can use the same materials again.

My small boat can carry the seed, but it cannot carry Pip. Load the seed at the yellow launch. Steer around the rock to my blue landing. I will come to collect the seed and bring it to our planting spot. You may also carry the seed across the secured bridge.

Keep the boat’s route clear. When you reach the garden, prepare our planting spot with me, place the seed and cover it with soil. The bridge stays in place so everyone can return.

The seed stays dark in a boat or a hand. Its lantern-flower grows only after its roots reach the garden’s soil.
