## What the map means for play and evidence

The story/action situation gives each focus word a purpose. Children can request meaning, hear the word in context, revisit help and apply an idea in an explanation or action. Synonyms and clear everyday explanations remain valid. No story branch or optional old page is forced to ensure every target word is encountered a fixed number of times.

Actual word occurrences, opportunities to apply an idea, displayed sources, requested definitions, model audio and the child's response remain distinct. Successful action may follow observation or experimentation. A lookup or repeated model answer is assistance, not independent mastery. A supported response can demonstrate meaning in that moment; improvement requires separate R20 evaluation.
