- Capture Tony's actual answer before translating it into requirements. A quotation must be exact; label a paraphrase as a paraphrase. Use a date and this task's conversation reference, plus enough wording to locate the agreement.
- Separate current observed behavior, historical design intent, Tony's feedback, assistant interpretation, and proposals. A build-status statement is documented evidence, not a fresh playthrough observation.

- Record an agreed choice to keep existing behavior as carefully as a change. Identify the applicable existing text or contract.
- If part of an answer is settled and part is open, separate the records or list the unresolved part explicitly. Do not mark the whole topic complete prematurely.
