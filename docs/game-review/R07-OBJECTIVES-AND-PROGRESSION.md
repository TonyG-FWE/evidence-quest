## Progress follows events

The shown first-encounter table and exact copy live in [D034](D034-GOALS-AND-NEXT-STEPS.md). Later preparation, changed-plan, gathering, and ending guidance live in [D035](D035-CHAPTER-PROGRESSION-AND-ENDING-GUIDANCE.md). These are conditional routes, not a compulsory order of visits.

- A usable crossing permits travel; it does not move Pip automatically or plant the seed.
- Acquiring a page does not deliver or share it. The planting promise remains until actual planting with Grandma.
- Sol's agreed prepared ending, chosen written ending, or original draft is retained. Preparing and later sharing are separate.
- Time choice, role agreement, notice, Grandma report, actual arrival, and story contribution remain distinct. The changed-time example directs the actual missing notice without resetting other work.
- Begin the gathering follows the applicable preparation; future arrival, performance, or Grandma's later copy delivery cannot be prerequisites to beginning.
- Sol may leave his draft unfinished while fulfilling his contribution.
- If Mara attends, the accepted closing finishes in the garden. If absent, Pip delivers Grandma's actual story and may finish at the dock.
- Finishing the chapter and actually watching/narrating its studio ending remain separate events.

Alternate visits from D023-D025 remain supported. A direct later/Mara route can have her supply her story at the gathering without an invented early page handoff. Help reflects the player's actual progress and does not transfer knowledge to a character. There is no score, clue count, mandatory lookup, or inferred literacy gain in these guidance decisions.
