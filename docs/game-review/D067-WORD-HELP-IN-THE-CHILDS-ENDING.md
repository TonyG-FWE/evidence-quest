## Source and availability boundaries

A prepared or local meaning may be used only if it fits the child's actual use. The word's appearance elsewhere in the fixed story is not enough to justify copying that occurrence's meaning into this new sentence. Insufficient context, alternate senses and invented or misspelled words still need detailed handling.

If AI help is unavailable and there is no appropriate stored meaning for this use, agreed response:

> Word help isn't available for this word right now. Your writing is unchanged.

Retain the actual paragraph, any available pronunciation support and the normal return/writing actions. Do not invent a definition, substitute an unrelated story-glossary meaning or silently rewrite the word. This is an explicit coverage limit for new child-written words; it does not reduce D066's required local coverage of fixed text.
