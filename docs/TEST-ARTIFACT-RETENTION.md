# Test artifact retention

On September 16, Tony requested committing and pushing everything required for the game, with unnecessary testing records removed to free space. Source, runtime assets, original design files, test programs and setup instructions remain in the repository.

Generated browser recordings, trace archives, screenshots, raw browser output and duplicate source/build snapshots are excluded from future commits. The approved cleanup removed 299 verified Playwright trace archives and per-test video recordings, freeing 6,669,862,038 bytes (about 6.7 GB). Trace archives were identified by their actual trace entries; videos were checked for their container header. The [removed-file inventory](../evidence/repository-checkpoint-20260916/removed-playwright-recordings.json) records their paths and sizes.

Automatic approval review rejected the broader removal of historical snapshots. Those snapshots and two incomplete recording archives remain local and excluded from the game commit. No source history or original artwork was deleted. This narrower cleanup does not require Git LFS and makes no account or billing changes.

Small verification summaries are retained to preserve the actual results, initial failures and open qualification gaps. Their historical links and hashes can refer to raw artifacts no longer available in the repository. A retained result is a dated observation, not a claim that the deleted raw capture can still be inspected. Historical archive-reconstruction scripts may need those captures; normal build and test commands do not.

The following records have active dependencies and remain available:

- `evidence/group-6-review-20260915/group-5-migration-inputs.json` and `evidence/group-7-review-20260915/group-6-migration-inputs.json`: authentic saved-game fixtures consumed by current contracts and browser tests.
- `evidence/er13/production-art-exports.json` and `evidence/er13/art-runtime-inventory.json`: verified artwork cache and original-asset integrity checks.
- `evidence/group-7-finale-20260915/content-manifest.json`: generated content manifest and its current build output directory.
- Original import receipts and the `parent-import-history` / `import-history` folders: preserved authoritative source bytes.
- `evidence/er13/live-evaluation/`: the persisted provider-attempt history, including the unchanged halt at exactly 1/75. Cleanup must never reset that ledger.

The September 19 demo package adds a finite source boundary for the personal review profile. `assets/demo/manifest.json` declares every packaged file, its SHA-256, size, source path and exact hydration targets. The packet contains 151 files (585,710,767 bytes): 128 review runtime files plus their manifest, nine personal landscape derivatives, two provenance receipts, two path metadata files, five loader-verified original GLBs, the supplied-original inventory and three texture decoder files. This is a byte-identical selection, not a regenerated or quality-reduced copy. The remaining supplied originals are preserved locally; the inventory records all 143 originals without implying every original is needed by this build.

`npm run build:demo` hydrates the declared files into `.cache/final-demo-review/assets`, the selected `output/personal-landscape-20260918` and original-source paths, the two required evidence metadata files, and `public/garden-assets/basis`. It verifies existing targets and refuses to overwrite a mismatch. The clean-checkout packet covers the direct `path-support.json` runtime import and both personal asset loaders' original-hash checks. These are real build dependencies under `output/` and `evidence/`; the earlier claim that no game build reads those paths no longer applies. The normal production approval gate is unchanged.

`public/audio/cast/manifest.json` and its declared MP3/WAV files are tracked authored playback assets. Compact authored-generation records under `evidence/cast-audio-20260919/` and the durable `evidence/demo-ai-20260919/openai-attempts.jsonl` ledger retain their separate provenance. The prior `evidence/voice-ai-demo-20260918/openai-attempts.jsonl` remains part of the total recording allowance. Never reset provider accounting as a cleanup step. Generated audio for player-written text, request caches and credentials stay private under ignored `.cache/cast-audio-dynamic/` and `.env.server.local`.

New `.gitignore` rules exclude the generated `output/` tree, hydrated `public/garden-assets/`, recording and trace archives, and named duplicate candidate source/build trees. They do not delete or untrack originals, existing history, active saves or existing receipts. Do not stage all ignored files with a broad force-add. Stage only the finite demo manifest's files, its documentation/verification receipt, the packaging scripts, the declared authored-audio files and separately reviewed compact evidence. `.gitattributes` disables line-ending conversion for `assets/demo/**` and `public/audio/cast/**` so a Windows checkout preserves their hashes.

Historical full-original verification, source reconstruction and playback-recording helpers can still require local files outside the finite demo packet. Their old paths and hashes remain truthful provenance; a clean checkout is not claimed to reproduce every historical capture. The playable package, its current build, current tests and each dated evidence claim have separate boundaries. See [the current README](../README.md) for the reproducible local demo commands.
